2. The cost of a call in words (row 10, pick R7-P12), case by case

The words say the change of the two counters after each call. The numbers are those of the model: model.js:139 (a pull: +2, and +1 more when the engine stands in a track after a spot), model.js:137 (a pull: +1 coupling), model.js:156 (a spot: +1, and +1 more after a spot; the engine then stands in the track), model.js:151 (a spot onto a track that holds cars: +1 coupling), model.js:198 (a kick: +1, and +1 more after a spot; no coupling, model.js:196) and model.js:223 (secure: +0). The state "the engine stands in a track after a spot" is the field out of the model (false after a spot). The pull-out of a spot counts at the next call that moves the engine, so the cost words of that call name it: "the pull-out of your last spot".

Each example was played in the built app (scratch probe of row 10). Each change of the readout is the number in the words.

One number or more is singular: "+1 move", "+1 coupling". The words name a move of the engine as the model counts it (model.js:227-231); they do not say what a crew does on the ground.
