# The words of job Y4a (Wave 2c): the Yard access layer

Each new or changed learner-facing word of job Y4a, by row of the brief (`2c-plan.md`, C3). The
owner approves the words at the end of the wave. No row of this job is marked "(words)" in the
upgrade list; each new word is here, because each new word needs the owner's look.

No word here states, corrects or cites a rule. A refusal of the model (`model.js`, frozen) and each
banner of today keep their words: the status region says them as they are. "N", "k" and "m" stand
for a number that the app counts; "<title>" is the title of a job in `puzzles.js`; "<mark>" is a
car mark of a job, for example "CN 411002".

The SME reads the words of the text mirror and of the switch names before the change reaches `main`
(`MIRROR-WORDS`, `lead-rulings.md` section 4). The words were not checked by the SME.
